Work / Case study

How Retoldly turned a 17-point AI search newsletter into a 23-slide founder carousel.

Client: Favour AgariFounder: what's the context?🤠Senior Content Editor at ScalerrsPrimary platform: LinkedInService: Fully Retold
17

content signals in the original newsletter

23

slides in the finished carousel

Favour had already done the hard intellectual work.

Her newsletter broke down research from Kevin Indig, Semrush, Wix Studio, Ahrefs, Seer Interactive and AirOps into a practical checklist for AI-search content.

The source had depth, data and a clear point of view. My job was to translate that depth into a format people could move through easily on LinkedIn.

The challenge

The newsletter was useful, but dense.

It carried 17 content signals, multiple studies, statistics, source references and practical examples. Favour wanted each signal to keep its own space in the carousel, so the work needed to be long without feeling heavy.

The goal was to preserve the thinking, sharpen the delivery and make the research easier to consume visually.

The process

How the newsletter became a carousel

01

Study the source and her voice.

The newsletter was practical, research-aware and conversational. I kept that tone while rewriting the copy for slides.

02

Decide what deserved space.

All 17 signals stayed. Repeated context was cut, useful proof stayed, and the strongest numbers became visual anchors.

03

Build each signal for the swipe.

Each signal received a clear header, enough explanation to stand on its own and a practical takeaway where it helped the reader.

04

Design around the research.

Charts, callouts and visual grouping were used where they made the ideas easier to understand. The design followed Favour’s brand direction.

The finished work

23 slides, 17 signals, one consistent visual system

The final carousel included source references, data-led visual moments, a founder introduction and a closing CTA for Favour’s newsletter and services.

Why this matters

Strong long-form thinking can travel further when it is prepared for the format.

Favour did not need to spend hours deciding how to condense the newsletter or how every slide should look. The source remained recognizable while the structure changed for LinkedIn.

LinkedIn was the primary platform. The finished design can be adapted for Instagram when needed.
