Most founders on LinkedIn are caught between two imperfect options. Hire a ghostwriter, and you get polished text, sometimes with generic visuals. Hire a designer, and you get beautiful slides, but no guarantee that the content actually holds up editorially.

The result is sloppy: Carousels that look good but say nothing. Or content that says the right things but gets ignored because nobody designed it to be seen.

Retoldly bridges the gap. Every piece of content goes through an editorial review before it's ever designed. The message has to be right before the design can do its job.

Yes, AI tools can generate carousels. But they can't replace the judgment of someone who has spent seven years editing content for B2B brands and knows exactly what a founder's audience needs to read.

The Process

Simple by design.
Intentional by instinct.

01

You share your idea

Send a brief, rough notes, a long-form post, or even a voice note. There's no perfect format. All we need is your thinking, however it arrives.

02

Editorial meets design

Your content is reviewed, restructured if needed, and built into a carousel or infographic. Your voice stays intact, and your audience remains in mind throughout.

03

You receive, you publish

A publish-ready visual asset is delivered to your inbox. No back-and-forth or prompting, no learning curve. We give you content that's ready to go live.

Why it matters

AI can generate a carousel.
It still needs an editor.

AI can draft slides. It can even revise them. What it can't do is apply the human judgment that catches hallucinations, spots inaccuracies, and questions whether the idea actually holds up for your audience.

That's the editorial layer. It's what human oversight looks like in practice. And it's what comes standard with every piece of work from Retoldly.

About

Led by an editor
who designs with intention.

I've spent over seven years inside B2B brands and SaaS companies doing one thing: making sure content says the right things, to the right people, in the right way. I've led content teams, coached writers, and edited more LinkedIn posts than I can count.

What I noticed along the way is that the founders who struggled most on LinkedIn weren't struggling because they lacked ideas, but because their ideas were trapped in formats that nobody saves, shares, or comes back to.

A well-designed carousel triggers rapid distribution. And getting to a well-designed carousel requires someone who understands both what to say and how to make it impossible to ignore visually. That's the gap Retoldly fills.

When you work with Retoldly, you're not hiring a designer who happens to write. You're working with an editor who designs, and those two things in the same hands make a measurable difference to how your content performs.
